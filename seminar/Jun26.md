# Separable modelling of dynamic signed networks
Alberto Caimo, University College Dublin, Ireland, Thu 25th June 2026 at 13:00 UTC+1.

Signed networks represent both positive and negative relationships between actors, providing a powerful framework for studying systems in which cooperation and conflict jointly shape network evolution. In this talk, We present a separable temporal generative model for signed networks based on multilayer exponential random graph models. The approach assumes conditional independence between interaction and sign processes, preserving the flexibility and interpretability of binary network models while remaining consistent with principles of structural balance theory. Parameter inference is conducted within a fully Bayesian framework using an adaptive approximate exchange algorithm to address the doubly intractable likelihood. We illustrate the methodology through an analysis of signed relationships among US Senators during Ronald Reagan’s second term (1985-1989), examining the extent to which political voting behaviour exhibits stable and balanced patterns over time.
